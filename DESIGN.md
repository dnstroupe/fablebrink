# Cinema revision

The first version felt like an illustrated portfolio catalog. This revision makes discovery and watching the center of the experience.

## Decisions

Dark cinema areas contrast with a warm light catalog. DM Serif Display gives titles character. DM Sans keeps controls simple. Lime is reserved for key actions and emphasis.

Curated collections introduce a feeling. Wide and portrait artwork create different rhythms. Dedicated title pages carry the synopsis, editorial note, availability, related stories, and save action. Availability stays beside viewing actions; project explanations move to the footer and case study.

Two previews use original animated footage with sound. The train scene was trimmed after an inconsistent extra figure appeared in its last shot. The background can play muted, with an explicit pause control. Reduced motion stops it. Foreground viewing starts only by request.

Related titles use shared genre or mood, not a claimed personalization model. No invented ratings, studio credits, awards, or release dates.
